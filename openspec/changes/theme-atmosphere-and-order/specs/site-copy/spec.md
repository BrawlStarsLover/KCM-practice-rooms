## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: First house rule names the entrance door
The first house rule SHALL read "The entrance door locks 5 min after the period starts.", in every theme and in the rules pop-up. It replaces the earlier "Doors lock 5 min after the period starts."

#### Scenario: Rules on the page and in the pop-up
- **WHEN** the house rules are shown, on the page or in the pop-up, in any theme
- **THEN** the first rule reads "The entrance door locks 5 min after the period starts."
